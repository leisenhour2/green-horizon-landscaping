About Green Horizon Landscaping:

At Green Horizon Landscaping, we believe a beautiful outdoor space begins with quality work and dependable service. As a family-owned and operated business, we've proudly served homeowners and small businesses for over eight years, helping create and maintain landscapes that our customers can enjoy year-round.

Whether it's routine lawn maintenance, seasonal cleanups, hedge trimming, mulching, or complete flower bed installations, we approach every project with attention to detail and a commitment to excellence. Our team is fully licensed and insured, giving you peace of mind that your property is in capable hands.

We understand that every yard is unique, which is why we take the time to listen to your goals and recommend services that fit your property's needs and your budget. From the first consultation to the final cleanup, customer satisfaction is our top priority.

At Green Horizon Landscaping, we take pride in building lasting relationships through honest communication, reliable scheduling, and quality craftsmanship. When you choose us, you're choosing a team that treats your property as if it were our own.

We look forward to helping you create and maintain an outdoor space you'll be proud of.

Lawn Mowing:

Keep your lawn looking neat and healthy with our professional mowing services. We provide precise cutting, edging, and cleanup to give your property a clean, polished appearance.

Mulching:

Refresh your landscape with quality mulch that helps retain moisture, suppress weeds, and improve the appearance of your flower beds and garden areas.

Bush and Hedge Trimming:

Maintain the shape and health of your shrubs and hedges with expert trimming services. We carefully prune overgrown plants to enhance curb appeal and promote healthy growth.

Spring & Fall Cleanup:

Prepare your property for the changing seasons with comprehensive cleanup services. We remove leaves, branches, and debris to keep your yard clean, healthy, and ready for the months ahead.

Leaf Removal:

Don't let fallen leaves smother your lawn. Our efficient leaf removal service keeps your property looking tidy while protecting your grass from excess moisture and decay.

Flower Bed Installation:

Transform your outdoor space with professionally designed flower beds. We install attractive, low-maintenance landscapes that add color, texture, and value to your property.

Pressure Washing:

Restore the appearance of your home's exterior with professional pressure washing. We safely clean driveways, sidewalks, patios, decks, fences, and other outdoor surfaces, removing dirt, mildew, and grime.
